About

A consulting firm focused on decisions.

Every business reaches decisions that shape what comes next. We help owners make them with a clear view of the facts.

MSC Innovation LLC is a Florida company based in Sunny Isles Beach. We work with business owners and leadership teams who want an outside perspective before they commit to a direction.

Mission

To help businesses and business owners make better decisions through clear analysis and practical guidance.

Vision

To be a trusted partner for business owners at the moments that shape their company's future.
